Games for Change 2025

Empathy Engine: Social Impact Game

Theme: Social Justice Team: 3 Members 48-Hour Game Jam

A narrative-driven experience where players see the world from different socioeconomic perspectives. Built for the G4C Student Challenge.

Reflection:

The 48-hour deadline forced us to prioritize ruthlessly—we scoped to a 10-minute experience instead of something epic. I led technical implementation while teammates handled narrative and art. We used GitHub for real-time collaboration. We didn't place top three, but judges said our branching dialogue system was "surprisingly sophisticated for a game jam." I learned rapid prototyping under pressure and the value of diverse team perspectives.

Emoti-Con CS Fair 2025

Neural Nexus: AI Companion Experience

Theme: AI & Humanity Solo Entry 3 Months

A Unity simulation where players train an AI companion through positive reinforcement. Submitted to Emoti-Con NYC.

Reflection:

I had to present to judges with mixed technical backgrounds, so I practiced explaining why I chose specific algorithms in simple terms. I did a live demo and Q&A. I placed Top 10 of 200+ entries, with judges praising: "Excellent presentation skills and clear documentation of the development process." This taught me that communication is as critical as coding in game development.

InfyAPP Challenge 2025

EcoQuest: Environmental Awareness App

Theme: Sustainability Team: 4 Members 6 Weeks

A gamified web app tracking environmental actions. Applies game design principles (progression, rewards, feedback loops) to real-world behavior change.

Reflection:

I applied gamification beyond entertainment—daily challenges, streak mechanics, and progress tracking to motivate real behavior. We used Agile sprints with GitHub Projects for task management. The biggest lesson was ethical design: our initial reward system was too addictive. We added "healthy limits" celebrating consistency over intensity. This showed me that game designers have responsibility for player wellbeing.
