My Games

Games I built in Unity with gameplay, sound, and VFX.

Yggdrasil

Engine: Unity Time: 8 Weeks Team: Solo

You are the last survivor of a prehistoric tropical world. Navigate through jungles, gather resources, and rebuild civilization.

What I Built:

  • Open world exploration with jungle environments
  • Resource gathering system
  • Strategic competition mechanics
  • Survival and civilization-building gameplay

What I Learned:

Building an open world taught me how to manage large scenes in Unity. I learned to balance exploration with clear goals so players don't get lost.

Roll a Ball

Engine: Unity Time: 2 Weeks Team: Solo

Roll a ball around to collect points. Features collectible sounds, rolling sounds, win sounds, and VFX effects.

What I Built:

  • Ball physics and rolling controls
  • Collectible system with sound effects
  • VFX particle effects when collecting
  • UI buttons for reset, play, and instructions
  • Win screen after collecting all points

What I Learned:

This was my first Unity project with sound and VFX. I learned how audio and visual feedback make simple games feel polished and fun.

Gem Collector

Engine: Unity Time: 3 Weeks Team: Solo

Collect all the gems while avoiding harmful invasive crabs and obstacles to win.

What I Built:

  • Gem collection system with scoring
  • Enemy crab AI that patrols and chases
  • Obstacle avoidance gameplay
  • Win and lose conditions

What I Learned:

Adding enemies made me think about player challenge vs. fairness. I balanced crab speed and gem placement so the game is hard but not frustrating.

Jungle Adventure

Engine: Unity Time: 4 Weeks Team: Solo

Explore a jungle world, avoid dangers, and complete your adventure.

What I Built:

  • Jungle environment with exploration
  • Enemy and obstacle systems
  • Level progression and goals

What I Learned:

This project helped me practice level design. I learned to guide players through a space using lighting and object placement instead of just instructions.
